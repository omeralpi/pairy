# Security

Pairy runs on Stellar testnet only and holds no keys: a wallet key stays in the user's browser (encrypted under their pincode) or in Freighter, and the server never signs a transaction.

Report a vulnerability privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Please do not open a public issue for it.

Most useful to hear about: anything that lets a server, a stranger or the counterparty move funds the two traders did not agree to, read a key or pincode, sign in as someone else, or make the app record a trade differently from the chain.
